# Sales Performance Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project presents a Sales Performance Dashboard developed using both **Microsoft Excel** and **Power BI**. The dashboard analyzes monthly sales and profit performance and provides business insights to support data-driven decision-making.

## Tools Used

* Microsoft Excel
* Microsoft Power BI

## Objectives

* Analyze sales performance.
* Compare sales and profit trends.
* Identify the highest-performing month.
* Track key business metrics using KPIs.
* Visualize business performance through interactive dashboards.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

* Total Sales
* Total Profit
* Highest Sales Month
* Highest Profit Month

## Dashboard Features

* Monthly Sales Report
* Sales vs Profit Analysis
* KPI Tracking
* Interactive Visualizations
* Business Insights

## Business Insights

1. April recorded the highest sales (300) and profit (80).
2. January recorded the lowest sales (100) and profit (20).
3. Sales increased from January to April, showing positive business growth.
4. Profit increased along with sales, indicating healthy profitability.
5. April was the best-performing month in terms of both revenue and profit.
